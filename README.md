# Personal Developer Portfolio

A modern, responsive personal portfolio website built to showcase my projects, technical skills, academic background, and achievements in a clear and structured manner.

---

## 🚀 About the Project

This portfolio is designed as a central place for recruiters and collaborators to quickly understand who I am, what I know, and what I have built.  
It focuses on clean UI, smooth user experience, and well-organized content rather than unnecessary complexity.

---

## 👤 About Me

I’m **Krishan Raj**, an undergraduate student at **IIT Patna**, pursuing **Computer Science and Data Analytics**.

I am passionate about **web development and problem-solving**, where I combine clean design with logical thinking to build responsive, user-friendly applications. I enjoy working on real-world projects that focus on usability, performance, and scalability.

Currently, I am strengthening my foundations in **Data Structures & Algorithms**, exploring modern web technologies, and building projects that reflect both my technical skills and creative thinking. With a strong learning mindset, I aim to create impactful digital solutions that add real value.

---

## ✨ Key Highlights

- Clean and professional UI with consistent design
- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- Smooth scroll-based animations for better user engagement
- Clearly structured sections: About, Skills, Projects, Experience, and Contact
- Easy navigation with minimal and readable layout

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** – Semantic and accessible structure  
- **CSS3** – Modern layouts, animations, and responsive design  
- **JavaScript (ES6+)** – Interactivity and dynamic behavior  
- **AOS (Animate On Scroll)** – Smooth scroll-based animations  
- **UI/UX Principles** – Focus on readability, spacing, and visual hierarchy  

---

## 📂 Sections Included

- **Hero Section** – Quick introduction and role overview  
- **About Me** – Background, interests, and goals  
- **Skills** – Categorized technical skillset  
- **Projects** – Real-world projects with live demos and source code  
- **Experience & Education** – Academic journey and practical exposure  
- **Contact** – Easy ways to get in touch
